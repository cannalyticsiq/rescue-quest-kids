WHAT THIS VERSION DOES
- Removes emoji UI placeholders.
- Adds Phaser 3 as the scene/game engine.
- Pumpkin Patch is now a real interactive scene.
- Tap/click the path to move the dino.
- Tap actual pumpkins in the scene to answer the mission.
- Correct pumpkin triggers a celebration animation.
- Wrong pumpkin wiggles and gives feedback.
- Hidden egg is collectible and animates into the counter.
- Leaves drift gently through the scene.
- Inventory uses custom illustrated SVG icons.
- Dino uses custom SVG art, not emoji.
- Other worlds already use the Phaser canvas for movement and are ready for their own mechanics next.
